Essential Emergency First-Aid Checklist

Stroke (F.A.S.T.)

Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech slurring → Time to call 108 immediately.

Minor Burns

Cool under running cool (not ice-cold) tap water for 20 minutes. Cover loosely with clean non-stick gauze; avoid butter or toothpaste.

Choking / Fainting

For fainting, lay person flat and elevate legs if safe. For severe airway blockage, perform abdominal thrusts and call emergency services.
